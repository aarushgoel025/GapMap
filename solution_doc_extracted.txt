How to Submit
Each team submits one Google Drive folder link containing this document (as PDF), a 15-minute
demo video and the code, by Wednesday, 28 October 2026, 4:00 PM IST. Keep answers short
— a few lines, a table or a screenshot per section is enough. Evidence beats description:
wherever you can show a number, a screenshot or a JSON sample, do that instead of
explaining.
1. Create one folder in Google Drive named TeamName_RADIX_TalentMatch.
2. Upload into it:
– Solution_Document.pdf — this template, completed and exported to PDF
– Demo_Video.mp4 — the 15-minute video (Section 12)
– Code.zip — your whole project folder, zipped, with a README.md at the top level
(see Section 9). Do not include API keys.
– Screenshots (optional)
3. Share the folder: Share → General access → "Anyone with the link" → Viewer. Copy the
link.
4. Test the link in an incognito / private browser window. If it asks for permission, it is not
shared correctly.
5. Paste the link in Submission Details below, and send it through the submission channel
your coordinator shares. The Scrum Master submits once for the whole team.

• The deadline is 4:00 PM IST on Wednesday, 28 October 2026. Nothing added or
changed in the folder after that is considered; file timestamps will be checked.
• A link that does not open for the evaluators will not be evaluated.

7. Quality Evidence
This section shows that your tool gives correct answers, not just answers.
7a. Extraction accuracy (JD Analytics & Resume Parsing)
Before running your tool, a team member reads 2 JDs and 2 resumes by hand and writes down
the correct skill list for each. This is your "ground truth". Then compare the tool’s output against
it.
• Correct = skills the tool extracted that are also on the hand-written list
• Precision = Correct ÷ Extracted — how many of the tool’s skills were right
• Recall = Correct ÷ Ground truth — how many real skills the tool caught
